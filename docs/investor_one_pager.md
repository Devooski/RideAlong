# RideAlong Investor One-Pager

## Product
RideAlong is a real-time rideshare matching platform for drivers with open seats and passengers traveling in the same direction. The product focuses on route-based matching, safety, and simple seat confirmation.

## Problem
Many intercity trips happen with empty seats while passengers pay higher costs for buses, trains, taxis, or last-minute rides. Existing ride-listing platforms often require manual searching and do not always feel real-time or trust-centered.

## Solution
RideAlong lets drivers broadcast a trip and lets passengers find available seats based on route proximity, departure timing, rating, and destination fit. The MVP demonstrates the core flow from search to match selection and confirmation code.

## Why now
Travelers want affordable options, drivers want to offset fuel costs, and mobile-first tools make same-day matching practical.

## Revenue opportunities
- Booking/service fee per confirmed ride
- Verified driver subscriptions
- Premium route visibility
- B2B commuter and campus partnerships
- Safety and insurance partnerships

## Current state
The attached MVP is browser-compatible and built for investor demonstration. It includes responsive UI, simulated matching, booking confirmation, logo assets, and launch documentation.
